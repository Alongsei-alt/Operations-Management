# Operations Management Assignments

## Project Overview

This repository contains completed coursework for the **Operations Management** course.

The assignments focus on applying operations management concepts to practical business problems, including process quality, capacity utilization, and global supply chain management simulations.

## Main Topics

The repository covers the following topics:

- Process and quality analysis
- Data collection and process measurement
- Variation and quality improvement
- Capacity utilization in service management
- Product mix decisions
- Global supply chain management simulation
- Production quantity decisions
- Revenue and profit optimization

## Assignment Content

### Process and Quality

This part focuses on analyzing a real process or task. Data was collected to measure variation in a quality-related dimension, such as time, output consistency, or service performance.

The goal was to understand whether the observed variation indicated a quality issue and to suggest possible process improvements.

### Capacity Utilization in Service Management

This part involved running a service management simulation and identifying a product mix that maximizes revenue while keeping the system stable.

The analysis focused on capacity limits, work-in-process behavior, and the logic behind choosing the preferred product mix.

### Global Supply Chain Management

This assignment was based on the Global Supply Chain Management simulation.

The task was to make supply chain decisions, such as production quantities and strategy adjustments, and explain the rationale behind these decisions. The final evaluation considered both simulation performance and the quality of the written explanation.

## Deliverables

The completed work includes:

- Final PDF reports
- Supporting Excel files
- Collected data
- Simulation results
- Charts and tables
- Written explanations of decisions and findings
